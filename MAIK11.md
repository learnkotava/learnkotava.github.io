### 11.2. Verbal affixes

These are the affixes for forming verbs. Mostly prefixes, with three suffixes.

Some examples of these types of affixes:

| Affix                       | Type     | Meaning                                                      | Usage                                                        |
| --------------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **-ú**                      | *Prefix* | to practice an activity, a sport                             | Added to a noun, to make an intransitive verb<br/>- **lexa** (music) ➡️ **lexú** (to play music, make music)<br/>- **utí** (sport) ➡️ **utiú** (to play a sport / sports) |
| **-wé**                     | *Prefix* | makes a transitive verb intransitive                         | Added to a transitive verb, to make it intransitive<br/>- **betá** (to change (sth)) ➡️ **betawé** (to change (oneself); become different) |
| **-sí**                     | *Prefix* | makes an intransitive verb transitive                        | Added to a transitive verb, to make it intransitive<br/>- **lubé** (to fall) ➡️ **lubesí** (to drop)<br/>- **kenibé** (to sleep) ➡️ **kenibesí** (to put to sleep) |
| **ko-...-(á)**<sup>1</sup>  | *Suffix* | idea of introducing, bringing in, entering                   | Added to a noun, to make a transitive verb<br/>- **tota** (boat) ➡️ **kototá** (to board) |
| **div-...-(á)**<sup>1</sup> | *Suffix* | idea of taking out, extracting, exiting                      | Added to a noun, to make a transitive verb<br/>- **direm** (car) ➡️ **divdiremá** (to get out of a car) |
| **tu...-á**<sup>2</sup>     | *Suffix* | idea of making something a certain way, putting it in a certain state | Added to a noun, to make a transitive verb<br/>- **winugaf** (official) ➡️ **tuwinugá** (to make sth official)<br/>- **listaf** (beautiful, nice) ➡️ **tulistá** (to beautify, embellish) |
| **gri-...-á****<sup>2</sup> | *Suffix* | idea of ending a state, taking something out of a state      | Added to a noun, to make a transitive verb<br/>- **tuwavaf** (natural) ➡️ **grituwavá** (denaturalize) |

> <sup>1</sup> : If ending in consant or in **-o**, the vowel **-á** is added as a necessary verb ending.
>
> <sup>2</sup> : Must always add an -**á** verbalizer to the noun root.

### 11.3. Affixes to Verbs

These are the affixes (all suffixes) for forming other types of words (nouns, determiners) from a verb root.

Some examples of these types of affixes, leaving aside the specific suffixes that form participles (for participles, see [Lesson 14](MAIK14.md)):

| Affix    | Type     | Meaning                                    | Usage                                                        |
| -------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **-ks**  | *Suffix* | result from the action of a verb           | Makes nouns<br />- **warzé** (to produce) ➡️ **warzeks** (a product) |
| **-nik** | *Suffix* | person undergoing an action                | Makes nouns<br />- **libú** (to elect) ➡️  **libunik** (elected official; chosen one) |
| **-ra**  | *Suffix* | verbal action (verbal noun)                | Makes nouns<br />- **betá** (to change) ➡️ **betara** (change (n.))<br />- **budé** (to close) ➡️ **budera** (closing (n.)) |
| **-sik** | *Suffix* | agent, person performing an action         | Makes nouns<br />- **warzé** (to produce) ➡️ **warzesik** (producer) |
| **-xa**  | *Suffix* | object affected by an action               | Makes nouns<br />**- gluyá** (to link, connect) ➡️ **gluyaxa** (link) |
| **-xe**  | *Suffix* | building or location of an action          | Makes nouns<br />**- iá** (to manufacture, build) ➡️ **iaxe** (factory, plant) |
| **-xa**  | *Suffix* | place where an action unfolds, takes place | Makes nouns<br />- **koblí** (to be born) ➡️ **koblixo** (place of birth) |

| Affix     | Type     | Meaning                                  | Usage                                                        |
| --------- | -------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **-ak**   | *Suffix* | container, object that contains          | **- eip** (salt) ➡️ **eipak** (salt shaker)                   |
| **-cek**  | *Suffix* | contents, thing contained within         | - **art** (mouth) ➡️ **artcek** (a mouthful)                  |

| **-opa**  | *Suffix* | science, technology                      | - **ava** (language) ➡️ **avopa** (linguistics)               |
| **-tum**  | *Suffix* | small group, sheaf, tuft, handful of sth | - **usuk** (hair) ➡️ **usuktum** (lock or tuft of hair)       |
| **-ugal** | *Suffix* | time, epoch, era, season                 | **- imwa** (flower) ➡️ **imwugal** (flowering period)         |
| **-xa**   | *Suffix* | object, material                         | **- inta** (wood) ➡️ **intaxa** (object made of wood)         |
| **-xe**   | *Suffix* | building affected by a thing's use       | **- namulol** (sheep) ➡️ **namulolxe** (sheepfold)            |
| **-xo**   | *Suffix* | place, space                             | - **moava** (gold) ➡️ **moavaxo** (gold mine)                 |
| **-ya**   | *Suffix* | female being                             | **- tavesik** (professor) ➡️ **tavesikya** (female professor) |
| **-ye**   | *Suffix* | male being                               | - **jaftol** (bovine) ➡️ **jaftolye** (bull)                  |

*Note: Suffixes are added directly to the noun.  When a noun ends in **-a**, that last letter drops in favor of the initial vowel of the suffix.*  e.g. **imwa** ➡️ **imwugal**

As for noun suffixes that make determiners, here are the 4 most common (out of a total 8):

| Affix                   | Type     | Meaning                                        | Usage                                                        |
| ----------------------- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **-af, -f**<sup>3</sup> | *Suffix* | qualitative adjective, having the character of | **- tawaday** (countryside) ➡️ **tawadayaf** (rural)          |
| **-iskaf**              | *Suffix* | being deprived of                              | - **usuk** (hair) ➡️ **usukiskaf** (bald)                     |
| **-kiraf**              | *Suffix* | containing, carrying                           | **- eip** (sel) ➡️ **eipkiraf** (salty)                       |
| **-koraf**              | *Suffix* | having the form of, resembling                 | - **gamda** (cross) ➡️ **gamdakoraf** (cruciform, cross-shaped) |

> <sup>3</sup> *The form of the suffix **-f** is used when the noun ends in a vowel (other than -**a**).*

### 11.5. Affixes to Determiners

These are the affixes for forming nouns and adverbs of manner from a determiner.  There are only 5 such affixes.  These are added directly to the determiner root (i.e., with the typical **-af** or **-f** ending).

| Affix    | Type     | Meaning                                   | Usage                                                        |
| -------- | -------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **-aca** | *Suffix* | thing, action having the character of     | Makes nouns<br /><br />**- listaf** (beautiful, pretty) ➡️ **listaca** (a beauty, a pretty thing)<br />- **savsaf** (ancient, old) ➡️ **savsaca** (an ancient thing, an antiquity) |
| **-e**   | *Suffix* | part of a whole that has the character of | Makes nouns<br />- **listaf** (beautiful, pretty) ➡️ **liste** (the beautiful, a beautiful part of a whole)<br />**- savsaf** (ancient, old) ➡️ **savse** (that which is old, the old part of a whole) |
| **-ik**  | *Suffix* | person having the quality or character of | Makes nouns<br />**-listaf** (beautiful, pretty) ➡️ **listik** (pretty person, beautiful person)<br />**-savsaf** (ancient, old) ➡️ **savsik** (an elderly person) |
| **-on**  | *Suffix* | adverb of manner                          | Makes adverbs<br />**- listaf** (beautiful, pretty) ➡️ **liston** (beautifully, prettily)<br />**- savsaf** (ancient, old) ➡️ **savson** (in an ancient way, formerly) |
| **-uca** | *Suffix* | quality of                                | Makes nouns<br />- **listaf** (beautiful, pretty) ➡️ **listuca** (beauty)<br />- **savsaf** (ancient, old) ➡️ **savsuca** (oldness, seniority) |
