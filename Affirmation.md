## x. Affirmation

Like the negative particles (x.x), Kotava also has one for reinforcement, or being extra assertive, affirming or emphasizing the statement.

The affirmative is indicated by **en**.  **En** by itself means 'yes'.

​	**En estú.** I really am eating.

​	**Koe Paris en irubá.** I really do live in Paris. I *do* live in Paris.

​	**Van in en kiaval.** You're greeting him straightforwardly. You're 'straight up' greeting him.
