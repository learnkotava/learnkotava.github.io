## x. Exclamations: man, man-

Exclamations in Kotava must end in an exclamation mark, which takes a space before it.

You can make a simple sentence into an exclamation using the word **man**:

​	**Gazik tiyir.** He was the king.

​	**Man gazik tiyir !** What a king he was!

You can also use **man-** as a prefix to construct words using the elements in ([x.x.1](RelativePronouns.md)), to make pronouns:

​	**Mantan tir !** What a guy! He's quite a guy! He's some guy!

​	**Va mancoba askil !** What a thing you're doing! That's quite a thing you're doing! Isn't that something (the thing you're doing)!

Or to make adverbs:

​	**Maneke kobal !** You're working so much!

​	**Manlize irubayá !**  I lived in such a place! What a place it was I lived in!
