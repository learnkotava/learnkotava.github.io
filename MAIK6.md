### 6.3. Exercises about Tenses

Below are some short phrases where you'll need to choose the correct tense agreement. Also some situations using one tense rather than another, depending on the context. Pronounce these phrases aloud to get started memorizing them.

| Kotava                                                       | Question                                                     | Answer                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1. **In va Francava dene kotla taveyer.**                    | The tense of this phrase is:<br />A. Present<br />B. Past<br />C. Future | B.<br /><br />He taught French at the university.            |
| 2. **koe bata mawa fenteté**                                 | The tense of this phrase is:<br />A. Present<br />B. Past<br />C. Future | C.<br /><br />I will be cold in this room.                   |
| 3. **va kelda wital**                                        | The tense of this phrase is:<br />A. Present<br />B. Past<br />C. Future | A.<br /><br />You see the path.                              |
| 4. **va Francava vayav ise va kotla nobav**                  | The tense of this phrase is:<br />A. Present<br />B. Past<br />C. Future | A.<br /><br />We study French and attend university.         |
| 5. **ixam grupé da ko Amerika lapití**                       | The tense of this phrase is:<br />A. Present<br />B. Past<br />C. Future | C.<br /><br />I already know that I will go to America.      |
| 6. **ba bartiv ke sielestura den rin artlanitiv**<br />We will arrive at your house around dinner time. | Put this phrase in the past tense.                           | **Ba bartiv ke sielestura den rin <u>artlaniyiv</u>.**       |
| 7. **kotviele batlize tigil, pune jontik zvak sokid**<br />Every time you are here, many problems occur. | Put this phrase in the future tense.                         | **Kotviele batlize tigitil, pune jontik zvak sokitid.**      |
| 8. **ede koe iaxe co kobayac, pune blira lanon [co tiyir] figapafa**<br />If you had worked in a factory, then life would definitely have been very difficult. | Put this phrase in the present tense.                        | **Ede koe iaxe co kobac, pune blira lanon [co tir] figapafa.** |
| 9. **kas trakul da in va rinaf warzaf direm albatar ?**<br />Do you think he will like your new car? | Put this subordinate sentence in the past tense.             | **Kas trakul da in va rinaf warzaf direm <u>albayar</u> ?**<br />Do you think he liked your new car? |
| 10. **va Kotava vayal ise va kotara ava vulkul !!**<br />Study Kotava and forget about any other language! | Put this phrase in the present tense.                        | **Va Kotava vayal ise va kotara ava vulkul ‼**               |
| 11. the bird installed its nest in this tree                 | Translate this phrase into Kotava.<br /><br />Word bank:<br />**zveri** (bird)<br />**inké** (to install)<br />**begama** (nest)<br />**aal** (tree) | **Zveri va begama ko bal aal inkeyer.**                      |
| 12. Birds are animals capable of flying.                     | Translate this phrase into Kotava.<br /><br />Word bank:<br />**zveri** (bird)<br />**sulem** (animal)<br />**talá** (fly in the air)<br />**grutalá** (to be able to fly) | **Zveri tir grutalas sulem.**                                |
| 13. Birds fly solely during the day.                         | Translate this phrase into Kotava.<br /><br />Word bank:<br />**antaf** (only, solely) / **anton** (only)<br />**afix** (day and night) / **afizon de** (during the day) | **Zveri anton mileon talar.**                                |
