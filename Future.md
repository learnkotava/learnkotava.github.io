## x. Future: -t-

Kotava forms the future a lot like the [past](Past.md), only with -**t**-.  That is, the future is formed by inserting a simple suffix before the personal ending (**2.2**) of the verb: the letter **t-**, plus the vowel right before the **t**.

In other words, add -**t** + the vowel at the end of the stem (**2.1**):

| Verb Stem           | Present Tense        | Past Tense                  |
| ------------------- | -------------------- | --------------------------- |
| **danka** - to sing | **danká** - I sing   | **dankatá** - I will sing   |
| **dole** - to sell  | **doler** - he sells | **doleter** - he will sell  |
| **ti** - to be      | **tir** - she is     | **titir** - she will be     |
| **estu** - to eat   | **estud** - they eat | **estutud** - they will eat |

That's it!
